# Survivors-like / Bullet Heaven market on Steam, 2022 to October 2026

Research date: 13 October 2026. Sources are third-party estimators (Steam Page Analyzer, SteamPulse, GameDiscoverCo) plus publisher statements. Unit sales for indie titles are **estimates** unless a publisher or developer announced them; this is flagged per line.

Important framing fact: the official Steam tag "Bullet Heaven" did **not** exist in 2024. It was added by Valve on **19 May 2026** (new tag, not a rename), after a December 2025 community poll (8,023 responses, 450+ developers involved). Before that, the genre lived under "Action Roguelike", "Roguelite", "Shoot 'Em Up", "Top-Down Shooter" and user-applied tags. Any per-year count of "Bullet Heaven" games for 2022–2025 is therefore a **retroactive** count of games tagged after May 2026, and likely undercounts older years (devs of old/abandoned games rarely retag).

## 1. How many survivors-likes release on Steam per year?

### Takeaway
Release volume roughly doubled every year since 2022 and exploded in 2026: about 17 (2022) → 37 (2023) → 69 (2024) → 103 (2025) → 329 by 27 Sept 2026 (retroactive "Bullet Heaven" tag counts), with GameDiscoverCo counting 72 Bullet Heaven launches in August 2026 alone. Part of the 2026 jump is a tagging artefact (tag created May 2026), but even so the genre now sees well over one new release per day.

### Cited Findings
- Games tagged Bullet Heaven by release year: 2020: 1; 2021: 2; 2022: 17; 2023: 37; 2024: 69; 2025: 103; 2026 (to 27 Sept): 329, described as "+406% on the same weeks of 2025". Dataset: Steam store search, 1,318 games tagged Bullet Heaven, captured 27 Sept 2026 — [Steam Page Analyzer, Bullet Heaven niche](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven)
  - Caveat (my reading): the per-year counts sum to ~558, not 1,318. The site does not explain the gap (possibly unreleased/coming-soon games, games without a parsable release date, or a stricter filter for the yearly series). Treat the yearly series as an order of magnitude, not a census.
- "72 'Bullet Heaven' games launched on Steam in August 2026 alone" — [GameDiscoverCo, "When 1.0 goes nice: Yet Another Zombie Survivors edition", Sept 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/when-10-goes-nice-yet-another-zombie)
- Bullet Heaven tag added by Valve on 19 May 2026 as one of 17 new tags (definition: "The opposite of Bullet Hell; Focus on upgrades while automatically attacking hordes of enemies") — [GamingOnLinux, 19 May 2026](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/05/valve-make-adjustments-to-steam-tags-and-they-settled-the-vampire-survivor-like-argument-with-bullet-heaven/page=1/)
- December 2025 naming poll by Awesome Games Studio: 8,023 responses, "bullet heaven" won — [GamingOnLinux, Dec 2025](https://gamingonlinux.com/2025/12/the-results-are-in-bullet-heaven-moves-forward-to-be-an-official-genre); 450+ developers coordinated; Bullet Heaven Fest editions in 2023, 2024 (2.0) and 2025 (3.0) — [Wikipedia, Vampire Survivors–like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors%E2%80%93like)
- Genre origin: Magic Survival (mobile, 2018 KR / 2019 global) inspired Vampire Survivors; 2022 gold rush (HoloCure, Brotato, 20 Minutes Till Dawn) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors%E2%80%93like)

### Inferences
- At 72 launches/month (Aug 2026), a game launching in autumn 2027 should expect 60–100+ direct genre competitors in its launch month alone, before counting hybrids tagged otherwise.
- The 2026 surge is partly real (the tag gives a store page and a recommendation lane, which attracts low-effort entries) and partly retagging. Either way, the trend is up, not plateauing, in terms of supply.

### Gaps
- No reliable pre-tag (2022–2024) count from SteamDB/Gamalytic/VG Insights found; SteamDB and games-stats.com returned 403. No Zukowski post with a yearly release count.
- No per-year count for the old "Action Roguelike" tag to cross-check.

## 2. Review / revenue distribution: median outcome vs hits

### Takeaway
On a like-for-like cohort (released Jan 2024–Jun 2025, 107 games), survivors-likes do clearly better than the Steam average: 41% reach 100 reviews and 13.1% reach 1,000 (vs 17.3% and 4.2% for all of Steam); median 75 reviews vs 11. But the median game is still small (75 reviews ≈ 2,000–3,000 units ≈ low five figures gross), and the $5–9.99 bracket outperforms the sub-$5 bracket.

### Cited Findings
- Cohort Jan 2024–Jun 2025 (107 Bullet Heaven games) vs all Steam — [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven):
  - ≥10 reviews: 88.8% vs 53.2% (1.67×)
  - ≥100 reviews: 41.1% vs 17.3% (2.38×)
  - ≥1,000 reviews: 13.1% vs 4.2% (3.1×); "1,000 reviews is the usual rule of thumb for roughly $150,000 in gross revenue"
  - Median reviews: 75 (Bullet Heaven) vs 11 (all Steam); median list price $5.99 vs $4.99
- By price bracket (same cohort) — [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven):
  - Under $5: 46 games, 23.9% reach 100 reviews, 8.7% reach 1,000, median 37 reviews
  - $5–9.99: 42 games, 52.4% reach 100, 14.3% reach 1,000, median 141 reviews
  - $10–14.99: 12 games; $15–19.99: 2; $20+: 3 (too few for stats)
- Top Bullet Heaven games of the last 12 months by reviews (as of Sept 2026): BALL x PIT 15,516 reviews (95%, $14.99); Yet Another Zombie Survivors 5,598 (94%, $14.99); The Spell Brigade 5,030 (86%, $12.99) — [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven) (Megabonk presumably excluded by the 12-month window or tagging)
- Review-to-sales multiplier (Boxleiter method, **estimate**): ~30× for recent releases, working range 20–40×, error margin 30–50% — [Steam Page Analyzer blog](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-review-to-sales-multiplier)
- Example of a "good indie" outcome: Vital Shell (solo, part-time dev, launched 7 Jan 2026): **estimated** 61,398 units / $247,000 revenue, 1,505 reviews at 97% positive — [SteamPulse](https://edge-test.steampulse.org/game/3741860) (third-party estimate; implies ~41× multiplier). Zukowski: 17,826 wishlists at launch, 4,573 before Next Fest, +2,187 during Next Fest, +6,652 from Popular Upcoming, ~15 months of dev, Steam page 217 days pre-launch, demo median playtime 22 min, 8 days on New & Trending, a bundle with Vampire Survivors earned "enough to buy a decent car" — [howtomarketagame.com, 19 May 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)
- Mid-tier hit: Yet Another Zombie Survivors — ~750,000 PC copies, ~$5M gross (estimate by GameDiscoverCo), 3 years in Early Access, 1.0 on 20 Aug 2026, 93k copies in first two weeks of EA and 88k in first two weeks of 1.0, peak CCU 10,200 (EA weekend) / 9,900 (1.0 weekend), median playtime 11h12, price $10–15, buyers 29.5% China, 18.5% US, 2.2% France — [GameDiscoverCo, Sept 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/when-10-goes-nice-yet-another-zombie)
- Hits (announced by developers/publishers, i.e. hard-ish data):
  - Megabonk (solo dev Vedinad, 18 Sept 2025, Unity, 3D): 1M copies in 2 weeks; peak 117,336 CCU on Steam — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megabonk); peak "over 102,000" at the 2-week mark — [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/77426-reverse-bullet-hell-indie-game-megabonk-hits-a-million-copies-sold-in-two-weeks)
  - BALL x PIT (Kenny Sun & friends / Devolver, 15 Oct 2025): 100k in 24h, 400k in a week, 1M in ~6 weeks, 2M in ~10 months (excl. Game Pass) — [Push Square, Aug 2026](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/08/devolvers-brilliant-roguelite-dopamine-factory-ball-x-pit-is-a-smash-hit-at-2-million-sales); "more than tripled Devolver's full-year sales expectations in 6 weeks" — [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/56013-ball-x-pit-more-than-tripled-devolver-digital-s-full-year-sales-expectations-in-just)
  - Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor: 1M copies (Feb–Mar 2024, in Early Access); 76% of owners also own DRG (IP leverage) — [howtomarketagame.com, 8 Apr 2024](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/04/08/how-creating-a-spinoff-for-a-hot-genre-lead-to-success-for-deep-rock-galactic-survivor/)
  - Halls of Torment: 1M+ copies (Oct 2024), console ports announced 2025 — [GamingOnLinux, Oct 2024](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2024/10/halls-of-torment-hits-1-million-sales-more-free-updates-planned/comment_id=271105); [iXBT, Sept 2025](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2025/09/03/indi-xit-v-stile-vampire-survivors-skoro-doberetsia-do-playstation-i-xbox-prodan-million-kopii-halls.html)
  - Brotato: 10M+ copies by 2025 (all platforms, incl. mobile) — [Wikipedia, Brotato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brotato)

### Inferences
- Rough translation of the cohort figures with a 30× multiplier (estimate): median game ≈ 2,250 units; median $5–9.99 game ≈ 4,200 units (~$25–35k gross before Steam cut/refunds/regional pricing); 1-in-7 games in the $5–9.99 bracket reaches ~30k units (~$150k gross).
- The cohort predates the 2026 supply explosion (329 releases in 9 months). Success rates for 2026 releases are very likely lower than 13.1%/41.1%, since demand did not grow 4×. No 2026 cohort data exists yet to confirm.
- Vestiges' planned 5–10 € price sits in the bracket that historically performed best for the genre.

### Gaps
- No Gamalytic/VG Insights median revenue for the genre found (pages blocked or no published article). No reliable 500- and 10,000-review shares; 10,000+ appears limited to a handful of titles (BALL x PIT, Megabonk, VS, Brotato, DRG:S, Halls of Torment, YAZS no: 5.6k).
- Vampire Survivors lifetime Steam sales: only unverified figures found (not cited).
- No Reddit postmortem with hard numbers for a failed survivors-like found in the searches.

## 3. Saturated or still growing? Fatigue vs demand, analyst views

### Takeaway
Both are true: demand for the genre is still very strong at the top (Megabonk and BALL x PIT were among the biggest indie launches of 2025, Vampire Crawlers hit 100k wishlists in 48h), but supply has grown much faster than demand and analysts now describe the genre as mature and oligopolistic, viable only with high execution quality and a strong hook. "Players play bullet heavens but don't anticipate them" (Zukowski), which hurts wishlist-driven launch strategies.

### Cited Findings
- Zukowski (May 2026): Vital Shell "demonstrates that Bullet Heaven is still a viable genre for small teams who can deliver excellent quality, but it is no longer a quick-win-rapid-release genre"; players "don't anticipate them — they don't track and follow them"; success depends on launch quality and word-of-mouth more than pre-launch hype; about one year of dev minimum; not recommended for first-time developers; recent successes cited: Monsters are Coming!, Shard Squad, GOD FORSAKEN, TerraTech Legion, Grind Survivors — [howtomarketagame.com, 19 May 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)
- Zukowski (Nov 2025), "cycle of a hit genre": phase 3 (fragmented second wave: Brotato, Rogue Genesia, Nomad Survival, Soulstone Survivors) → phase 4 (polished consolidation by bigger studios/IP: DRG Survivor, Temtem Swarm, Warhammer Survivors). In May 2025, three games (DRG:S, Vampire Survivors, Brotato) held "over 75% of the market share" (oligopoly for over a year); Megabonk then "completely took over the genre" and "wiped out the share of the competition", possibly reopening the market — [howtomarketagame.com, 12 Nov 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/11/12/the-cycle-of-a-hit-genre/)
- Zukowski (Apr 2024, older): disputes the idea that the genre is stale; advice: "study every single game in your chosen genre", simple input + deep meta, IP leverage — [howtomarketagame.com, Apr 2024](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/04/08/how-creating-a-spinoff-for-a-hot-genre-lead-to-success-for-deep-rock-galactic-survivor/)
- GameDiscoverCo (2026): single-player "Vampire Survivors-y roguelites" listed as one of the few surging new subgenres on PC (search snippet of the newsletter; full context not verified) — [GameDiscoverCo newsletter archive](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/archive); "Survivors-likes aren't incredibly strong on consoles" — [GameDiscoverCo, Sept 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/when-10-goes-nice-yet-another-zombie)
- Megabonk out-peaked Vampire Survivors (117k vs 77k CCU) and outperformed Borderlands 4 in daily players at launch — [Wikipedia, Megabonk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megabonk); [WN Hub, MidHard Games CEO on 2025](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-49655)
- MidHard Games CEO (survivors-like studio, Adrenaline Rampage, FatalZone): "The Bullet Heaven (Survivors-like) genre continues to evolve", expects "new synergies of core mechanics, new metas, new settings" in 2026 — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-49655)
- Vampire Crawlers (Poncle, card-based dungeon crawler spin-off) reached 100,000+ wishlists within 48h of announcement — [GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/games-and-numbers-november-1-december); #4 most-played demo of Feb 2026 Next Fest — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-february-2026s-steam-next)
- Developer-blog view (low-authority source, a games studio's Medium): "a really saturated market" needing "really high fidelity and polish or some degree of pivot"; many 2D sprite games "look like reskinned brotato or vampire survivors" — [Choost Games, Medium](https://medium.com/@choost-games/the-bullet-heaven-genre-is-eating-gaming-from-the-inside-f06410e938c0)
- Big-IP entries keep arriving: Warhammer Survivors (Steam page lists a 10 Nov 2026 date per search snippet; not yet released at research date) — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669620/Warhammer_Survivors/)

### Inferences
- The genre has the profile of a mature hit genre: winner-take-most at the top, a healthy-but-modest middle (Vital Shell-type: tens of thousands of units), and a long tail. Entering is not irrational, but the bar is "top 10–15% quality" with a hook readable in one screenshot/GIF.
- The "players don't anticipate bullet heavens" point is critical for a Next Fest-centric plan: wishlist counts may look weak pre-launch even for a game that will sell; conversely, launch quality, demo, streamers and bundles with genre leaders drive results.
- The arrival of licensed IPs (Warhammer, DRG, Temtem) and Poncle's own spin-offs increases competition for the genre's visibility lanes (Bullet Heaven tag pages, festivals).

### Gaps
- No quantitative player-side demand series (e.g. total genre CCU over time) found; fatigue is asserted qualitatively.
- No Gamalytic blog post specific to the genre found.

## 4. Trends and sub-niches that succeed

### Takeaway
The 2025–2026 breakouts are hybrids or format shifts, not pure 2D reskins: 3D (Megabonk, Vital Shell's PSX look), genre mashups (BALL x PIT = Breakout + survivors + base-building; Vampire Crawlers = cards + dungeon crawler; Everything Is Crab = evolution roguelite), co-op (The Spell Brigade), IP spin-offs (DRG:S, Warhammer). $5–15 is the price band of successes. Early Access works for the genre (DRG:S, YAZS, Halls of Torment all went EA first).

### Cited Findings
- 3D: Megabonk "Vampire Survivors but 3D", procedural 3D maps with verticality — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megabonk); Vital Shell is a top-down 3D PSX/N64-looking mech bullet heaven — [SteamPulse](https://edge-test.steampulse.org/game/3741860)
- Mashup: BALL x PIT is a "brick-breaking, ball-fusing, base-building survival roguelite" — [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/08/devolvers-brilliant-roguelite-dopamine-factory-ball-x-pit-is-a-smash-hit-at-2-million-sales)
- Co-op: The Spell Brigade (co-op) among 2025's notable releases, 5,030 reviews at $12.99 — [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven); co-op titles strong in Feb 2026 Next Fest generally — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-february-2026s-steam-next)
- Spin-offs into other formats: Vampire Crawlers (cards/dungeon crawler) — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-february-2026s-steam-next); Temtem: Swarm (monster-taming hybrid) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors%E2%80%93like)
- Sub-genre differentiation already established: Brotato = wave-based arena, Halls of Torment = gothic, Diablo-like skill dodging, 20 Minutes Till Dawn = twin-stick aiming — [Choost Games](https://choostgames.com/blog/new-survivors-like-games-2026/) (low-authority blog)
- Open-world / exploration variants exist on Steam (Sea of Survivors: "explore an open world with mysterious curses"; R.I.P.: Diablo-style looting + survivors) but none surfaced as a documented hit — [Steam search results via web search](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669620/Warhammer_Survivors/) (no sales data found)
- Early Access: YAZS spent 3 years in EA and its 1.0 two-week sales (88k) nearly matched its EA launch (93k) — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/when-10-goes-nice-yet-another-zombie); DRG:S hit 1M while in EA — [howtomarketagame.com](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/04/08/how-creating-a-spinoff-for-a-hot-genre-lead-to-success-for-deep-rock-galactic-survivor/)
- Pixel art: in a pixel-art-games tag study, Roguelite showed 75 titles, a 2.53 "success index" and $469,465 median revenue (sample definition and date unclear) — [OP Game Marketing, "So You're Making a Pixel Art Game?"](https://opgamemarketing.substack.com/p/so-youre-making-a-pixel-art-game)

### Inferences
- A "moving forward / nomadic" survivors-like with a wall chasing the player (Vestiges' Erasure) is a format twist comparable in kind to the hooks that worked (3D, Breakout, cards), but I found no hit proving demand for forward-scrolling/open-map survivors specifically: it is an untested differentiator, not a proven niche.
- 2D pixel art is now the default "reskin" look players associate with clones; the art direction must read as distinctive (Zukowski's "strong aesthetic themes"). A melancholic post-apocalyptic tone is uncommon in a genre dominated by comedic/bright visuals, which can be a plus if conveyed in capsule and trailer.
- China is the largest buyer country for at least one mid-tier genre hit (29.5% for YAZS): Simplified Chinese localization is likely worth more than many European languages.

### Gaps
- No data comparing 2D vs 3D outcomes across the genre.
- No numbers for extraction, tower-defense or deckbuilding survivors hybrids beyond the named titles.
- No documented "forward-scrolling" or "chasing wall" survivors-like with sales data found.

## 6. Platform considerations: Steam Deck, consoles, mobile

### Takeaway
Steam Deck is a meaningful slice (7–10% of YAZS players), consoles are weak for the genre per GameDiscoverCo, and the genre originates from and remains crowded on mobile.

### Cited Findings
- YAZS: 7–10% of players on Steam Deck; console (PS + Xbox) sales forecast under 50,000 vs ~750,000 on PC — [GameDiscoverCo, Sept 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/when-10-goes-nice-yet-another-zombie)
- "Survivors-likes aren't incredibly strong on consoles" — [GameDiscoverCo, Sept 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/when-10-goes-nice-yet-another-zombie)
- Console ports are nonetheless common: Halls of Torment to PlayStation/Xbox (announced Sept 2025) — [iXBT](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2025/09/03/indi-xit-v-stile-vampire-survivors-skoro-doberetsia-do-playstation-i-xbox-prodan-million-kopii-halls.html); MidHard ported Adrenaline Rampage and FatalZone to consoles via publishers — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-49655); BALL x PIT on PS5 and Game Pass — [Push Square, Dec 2025](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/12/hit-ps5-roguelike-ball-x-pit-reaches-one-million-sales-free-content-updates-inbound)
- Genre's mobile origin (Magic Survival) and Apple Arcade still adding survivors titles in Oct 2026 (River City Ransom-themed) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors%E2%80%93like); [GameDiscoverCo archive snippet](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/archive)

### Inferences
- For a solo dev, Steam Deck Verified (controller support, readable UI at 800p, stable framerate in dense hordes) is a cheap, high-value target; console ports are a post-launch, publisher-led bonus rather than a business case.
- Mobile clones (Survivor.io-type F2P) set player expectations of the format as "free/cheap"; this supports the low price point.

### Gaps
- No genre-wide Steam Deck share or Deck Verified impact figures found beyond YAZS.
- No data on mobile clone volume.
